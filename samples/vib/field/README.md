# Field vibrator signatures

Four real vibrator signature records, as examples for New plot > Vibrator
signature. Unlike the rest of `samples/`, these are field recordings, not
synthetic data.

- `signature_4trace_a.sgy`, `signature_4trace_b.sgy`: 4 traces, 1 ms, 12 s.
- `signature_13trace_a.sgy`, `signature_13trace_b.sgy`: 13 traces, 0.5 ms.

Positions and recording dates in the headers were shifted, so they do not
say where or when the sweeps were made. The samples are unchanged.
